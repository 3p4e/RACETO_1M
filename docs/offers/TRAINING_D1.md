Line D of `docs/OPPORTUNITY_MAP.md`: the fastest cash, and the pipeline for lines A, B and C.
Every price below is a **hypothesis**. The pre-sale test in §6 decides it.

## 1. Why a QA head would buy it now
- **The EU AI Act already requires it.** Since **2 February 2025**, providers and deployers of AI systems must ensure "a sufficient
  level of AI literacy" of staff who use them (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 4). Any company where staff use ChatGPT, Copilot
  or a vendor's AI module is a deployer.
- **The GMP rules are changing.** The EU and PIC/S published drafts of a revised Annex 11, a revised Chapter 4 and a **new Annex 22
  (Artificial Intelligence)** on 7 July 2025; the consultation closed on 7 October 2025. QA teams need to know what is coming before
  the next inspection asks. *Check the status of the final texts on the day you sell: final adoption is not confirmed here.*
- **Inspectors already ask about it.** Data integrity (ALCOA+) and computerised-system control remain among the most common GMP findings
  (PIC/S PI 041-1). AI tools are the newest uncontrolled computerised systems on most sites.

**Positioning:** taught by a Master Pharmacist and former QP who builds and uses AI in regulated documentation every day. This
is practice, not slides.

## 2. Who it is for
Heads of QA/QC, QPs, validation/CSV engineers, data-integrity leads and lab managers in pharmaceutical, herbal, food-supplement and
cosmetics manufacturers and ISO/IEC 17025 laboratories. No coding needed. Not for cannabis-specific audiences (see `CLAUDE.md` §2).

## 4. Outline (online version: two sessions)
### Session 1: The rules and the risk
1. **Where AI already sits on your site** (exercise): build an inventory of every AI use, official and unofficial.
2. **The rule map:**
   - Annex 11 (2011) and the 2025 draft revision;
   - draft Annex 22: scope, intended use, test data, performance metrics, human review, change control, monitoring;
   - Chapter 4 (draft): hybrid and AI-generated records;
   - the EU AI Act: AI literacy (Art. 4) and when a system is high-risk;
   - the EMA reflection paper on AI in the medicinal-product lifecycle.
3. **Risk-based classification** with ICH Q9(R1) and GAMP 5 (2nd ed.): critical vs non-critical use. Which uses need validation,
   which need a procedure, and which need neither.
4. **Data integrity for AI:** ALCOA+ applied to prompts, outputs, model versions and audit trails (PIC/S PI 041-1).

### Session 2: Doing it
5. **Live demonstration** on a fictional company's data (`synthgmp`): an AI reviews 200 batches of certificates; we score it against the
   36 planted defects (missed out-of-specification results, a missing page, a sub-lot mix-up). Test data, acceptance criteria and
   the human-review step, done the Annex 22 way.
6. **The minimum control set** for generative AI in QA/QC: an approved-use policy, a training record, human review and sign-off,
   version control, and supplier qualification of the AI vendor.
7. **Workshop:** each participant drafts the AI-use procedure outline and the validation approach for one real use on their own site.
8. **Inspection readiness:** the 10 questions an inspector is likely to ask, and evidence that answers each.

### What participants take home
Templates to adapt (written fresh for this course, from public guidance only):
- an AI-use inventory and risk-classification sheet;
- an outline of an AI-use procedure (approved uses, review, records);
- a validation-approach skeleton for a non-critical and a critical AI use;
- an AI-literacy training record (EU AI Act Art. 4);
- an inspection question-and-evidence checklist.
