# Target list: 10 interviews in 30 days

Five manufacturers and five laboratories. **Interview outside North Macedonia first** (Serbia, Croatia, Bulgaria, Slovenia,
Bosnia and Herzegovina): it is a small industry, and you decide when your employer hears about this venture, not the grapevine.
Large groups (> 300 staff) are buyers of reference, not the ICP. Include at most one, to learn how big firms buy.

## How to fill the slots (public registers; verify every entry)
- **Manufacturers:** the EudraGMDP database (GMP certificates by country and site, eudragmdp.ema.europa.eu); national
  agencies (HALMED Croatia, ALIMS Serbia, BDA Bulgaria, JAZMP Slovenia, MALMED North Macedonia); trade fairs and
  pharma/supplement association member lists.
- **Laboratories:** national accreditation-body registers of ISO/IEC 17025 testing laboratories (ATS Serbia, HAA Croatia,
  BAS Bulgaria, SA Slovenia, IARM North Macedonia). Filter for pharmaceutical, food-supplement, herbal or contaminant testing.
- **The person:** search LinkedIn for "Head of QA", "QA Manager", "Qualified Person", "Lab Manager" at the company. Reach them
  through a mutual contact, a society event, or a short note asking for 20 minutes "for research, not sales".
- **Exclusion check (private, before every contact):** not a customer, supplier, contract laboratory or business associate
  of your employer. Do not write those names in this repository.
