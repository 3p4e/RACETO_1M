# The race to €1M: strategy and validation dossier

Working document of the venture. Interactive model: the "€1M GMP Practice Plan" page (Claude artifact).
Education, not financial, tax or legal advice.

## Thesis
A Master Pharmacist with QP and Head-of-QC experience, plus a supervised-AI document and data engine, delivers
EU-GMP quality work in about one sixth of a consultancy's time. Money comes from selling **outcomes**: a passed
inspection, a validated method, a released batch. Selling hours or software comes later.

## Constraints (fixed until a lawyer or written consent says otherwise)
- **Clean room** (see `CLAUDE.md`): no employer material, synthetic data only, the engine rewritten from a specification.
- **Market:** non-cannabis EU-GMP manufacturers and ISO/IEC 17025 laboratories. The employment contract carries
  competition and IP clauses; the details stay with the owner and his lawyer, not in this repository.
- **Productivity gate:** do not sell throughput until corrections per AI-drafted document are below 1.

## Model result with default assumptions (20.11.2026)
Year 3: €1.53M revenue, **€848k pre-tax: short of €1M**. Lowest cash point €7k (month 4).
- **Unit economics:** desk client LTV:CAC ~17:1, payback ~2 months; programme ~9:1; small package ~2:1 (an entry
  product, not the business).
- **Biggest single lever:** programme price +€20k, worth +€119k a year.
- **Assumptions doing the most work:** programme sites won per year; desk churn and client cap.

## Validate-idea flow: Checkpoint 1 (awaiting the owner's sign-off)
- **ICP:** a non-cannabis EU-GMP manufacturer or ISO 17025 lab, 20–300 staff, in the Balkans or the EU, facing an
  inspection, a certification, or the revised Annex 11 / new Annex 22.
- **Assumption 0 (check, don't test):** consent or legal clearance for that field.
- **Riskiest assumption:** these companies already pay outside firms for GMP-readiness and validation work, at prices that support the margins.
- **Experiment:** 10 Mom-Test interviews in 30 days. Ask about the last time they bought the work; don't pitch.
- **Pass:** ≥ 6/10 paid outsiders in the last 18 months; ≥ 4 name a price at or above the low end; ≥ 2 commit money
  (a deposit or a signed SOW).
- **Pivot:** the pain is real only for one segment, so narrow to it and re-run.
- **Kill:** ≤ 3/10 paid outsiders, or nobody commits money, so keep it as advisory side income below €150k.

## Kill criteria for the venture
- No legal route to selling in the chosen field → stage 1 only, side income.
- Fewer than 5 paid jobs within 6 months of launch → rethink the segment.
- Corrections per document stay above 5 → sell expertise, not AI throughput.
