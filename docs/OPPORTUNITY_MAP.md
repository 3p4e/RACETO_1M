## 3. The portfolio that can reach €1M
One brand, one engine, three revenue lines that feed each other:

1. **D (training)** → sells within weeks and puts the owner in front of QA heads.
2. **A + B (expert-signed output)** → the high-ticket revenue; the engine makes it profitable.
3. **C (verification desk)** → recurring revenue that makes €1M repeatable rather than heroic.

Illustrative year 3 (to be replaced by interview evidence):

| Line | Volume | Revenue |
|---|---|---|
| D training | 12 online cohorts × 25 seats × €500 + 10 in-house days × €5k | €200k |
| A programmes and packages | 5 sites × €120k + 20 packages × €12k | €840k |
| B regulatory and CMC writing | 4 capacity retainers × €10k × 12 + 30 documents × €6k | €660k |
| C verification desk | 15 clients × €3.5k × 12 | €630k |
| **Total** | | **≈ €2.3M revenue**; at a 45–55 % margin, that is **≈ €1.0–1.25M profit** |

H (video) runs only on a fixed 5 h/week budget, as a creative and side-income line, until it shows its own paying customer.

## 4. What to stop
- Restarting LIMS, planners and QMS creators. **One engine**, rebuilt clean, in this repository.
- Consumer micro-projects (K) and anything resembling L.
- New infrastructure toys that no customer pays for.
