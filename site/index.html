Why now

Part of the AI regulation is already in force

Since 2 February 2025, Article 4 of the EU AI Act requires companies that use AI systems to ensure their staff have sufficient AI literacy. A QC analyst tidying a deviation in a chatbot already makes the company a deployer.

In July 2025 the EU and PIC/S published drafts of a revised Annex 11, a revised Chapter 4 and a new Annex 22 on artificial intelligence. The questions they raise are the ones an inspector will ask: where is AI used, who approved it, how was it tested, and who reviews its output?

The status of the draft texts is checked and updated before every cohort.

The live test

Would your AI catch these?

A fictional company: 200 batches, over 800 laboratory certificates, nearly 4,000 results. Every name and number is invented; limits are modelled on Ph. Eur. texts. Hidden inside are 36 defects of the kind that reach inspectors.
